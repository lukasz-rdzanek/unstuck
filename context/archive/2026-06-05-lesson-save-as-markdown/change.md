## Notes

Parked roadmap item / Linear UNS-21 (Phase 1 of its two-phase rollout). A "Save as Markdown" button next to Mark-Complete that downloads the current lesson as a single attributed `.md` note — client-side, zero new dependencies, Workers-safe. PDF and LLM-summarization are explicitly deferred to a later slice. The "useful notes, not a course mirror" constraint is honored with an attribution header/footer + single-lesson (no bulk) export.
